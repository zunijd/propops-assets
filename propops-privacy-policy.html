PropOps-Guides Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

PropOps-Guides ("we", "our") publishes educational and promotional content about real estate operations and automation. This app connects to our own social media accounts (including Pinterest) solely to publish our own content (pins, posts) on our own behalf.

What we collect

We do not collect personal data from visitors, followers, or Pinterest users. This app does not read, store, or process data belonging to other Pinterest users or accounts.

What this app does

This application is used solely for personal/business API access to create and manage Pins and Boards on our own authenticated PropOps-Guides Pinterest account. It does not access, read, or process data belonging to other Pinterest users.

Data we may access

Only our own authenticated account's Pins and Boards, for the purpose of publishing our own promotional content.
